The maker
Self made with love.

S3lfmade Prii is a one-person crochet studio. Every plushie, keychain and bouquet starts as a ball of yarn and a hook, and ends up as something you can hug, clip to your bag or keep on a shelf for years.

Because nothing comes off a machine, no two pieces are exactly alike. That is the point.
